This was a cybersecurity awareness simulation.

A QR code can look official. A page can look polished. A promise can feel irresistible. None of those things prove that the destination—or the request for your data—is trustworthy.

Before the next scan, pause.

  • Preview the link. Check the destination and look for misspellings or unexpected domains.
  • Verify the source. A familiar logo or campus poster can be copied.
  • Question the ask. Does this page really need your personal information to deliver what it promises?
  • Walk away when unsure. Confirm with the organiser or visit the official website directly.

The trap isn’t always a fake link.
Sometimes, it’s the promise that makes you stop thinking.
